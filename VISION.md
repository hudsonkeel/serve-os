# Serve OS Vision

Mission

Make caregiving organizations dramatically easier to operate.

Core Principle

External systems execute work.

Serve OS understands work.

Everything centers around:

Residents

Employees

Communities

Relationships

The long-term objective is to provide a single operational environment where employees can begin and complete their work without thinking about which software performs each task.

Serve OS should become the front door for every employee every day.
