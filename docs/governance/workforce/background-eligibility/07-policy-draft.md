## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes a consistent, defensible framework for evaluating criminal background information for employment with Serve Caregiving. Its objective is to protect clients, families, employees, and the organization, while ensuring that background-related decisions are based on documented, consistent criteria rather than ad hoc judgment.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all applicants, employees, contractors, volunteers, and temporary staff who may have direct or indirect access to clients, client residences, protected information, or organizational assets. See [`00-purpose-and-scope.md`](./00-purpose-and-scope.md) for the precise boundary of what this policy does and does not govern.

## 3. Guiding Principles

1. **Resident and client safety is the primary consideration.** Where this principle is in tension with any other consideration in this policy, safety governs.
2. **Every background eligibility decision must be consistent and explainable.** The same findings must produce the same classification, regardless of who or what performs the evaluation, and the reasoning behind every classification must be documented.
3. **Serve Caregiving may adopt standards more restrictive than the legal minimum** where appropriate to protect its client population, consistent with applicable law.
4. **Final hiring authority rests with Serve leadership.** This policy governs background eligibility classification; it does not itself make hiring decisions (see [`01-background-eligibility-ontology.md`](./01-background-eligibility-ontology.md) §3.8).

## 4. Background Eligibility Classifications

Every completed background investigation resolves to exactly one of four classifications: **Eligible**, **Reviewable**, **Presumptive Disqualification**, or **Automatic Disqualification**. Full definitions are maintained in [`02-background-eligibility-classifications.md`](./02-background-eligibility-classifications.md); a summary:

| Classification | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Eligible | No findings raising a reasonable concern for client safety or organizational trust. |
| Reviewable | Findings that require individualized review before a final classification is reached. |
| Presumptive Disqualification | Findings that presumptively disqualify, subject to executive review. |
| Automatic Disqualification | Findings that categorically disqualify, with no discretionary review available. |

## 5. Offense Categories

The specific offenses driving each classification are maintained in [`06-offense-taxonomy.md`](./06-offense-taxonomy.md) and its machine-readable counterpart, [`offense-taxonomy.yml`](./offense-taxonomy.yml). A summary:

- **Automatic Disqualification:** violence, sexual misconduct, crimes against vulnerable persons, healthcare/trust violations.
- **Presumptive Disqualification:** felony theft, burglary, forgery, embezzlement, drug distribution, repeated domestic violence, felony DWI.
- **Reviewable:** simple possession, a single misdemeanor DWI, minor theft/shoplifting, disorderly conduct, trespassing, criminal mischief, non-violent resisting arrest.
- **Eligible:** traffic citations, parking violations, expired registration, and isolated municipal violations.

## 6. Review Workflow

Background investigations are evaluated through the deterministic sequence defined in [`05-review-workflow.md`](./05-review-workflow.md):

1. Normalize offense(s) from the background report.
2. Match against Automatic Disqualification criteria.
3. If no match, match against Presumptive Disqualification criteria.
4. If no match, evaluate against Reviewable criteria.
5. If no match at any prior step, classify as Eligible.
6. Document the rationale and retain the record for audit.

## 7. Individualized and Executive Review

Reviewable findings receive an individualized review considering the offense's nature, time elapsed, evidence of rehabilitation, employment history, role relevance, and any pattern of repeat behavior. Presumptive Disqualification findings are escalated to executive review, which may uphold or, in a documented exceptional circumstance, override the presumption. Automatic Disqualification findings receive no review. Full procedure: [`05-review-workflow.md`](./05-review-workflow.md) §4–6.

## 8. Recordkeeping

Every classification decision is documented and retained, including the offense(s) considered, the criterion matched, the classification reached, the reviewer or system involved, and the date. This record forms the organization's audit trail for background eligibility decisions. Specific retention duration requires legal review (see [`05-review-workflow.md`](./05-review-workflow.md) §3, Step 6).

## 9. Governance and Review Cadence

This policy is a living document, owned by Serve leadership in coordination with legal counsel. It should be reviewed at least annually, and immediately upon any material change in applicable law, and updated to reflect operational experience. Every substantive change to this policy is recorded in [`decision-log.md`](./decision-log.md).
