# The Serve Operating Constitution

*Adopted 2026-07-22. This Constitution supersedes and replaces the Serve Intelligence Constitution (adopted 2026-07-13) in its entirety. It is the highest governing document for Serve: its people, its leadership, its software, and every system built to serve alongside them. Where any design, policy, or decision conflicts with this document, the decision should change, not the other way around, unless this Constitution is deliberately and openly amended.*

## Preamble

Serve exists to help people flourish through every season of life.

We believe every person possesses inherent dignity, every relationship deserves attention, and every act of care is strengthened, not replaced, by wisdom.

We built Serve so that caring for people could remain the work that matters most. We remove unnecessary work so our team can serve with kindness, competence, and presence.

Technology serves people. People serve people.

This Constitution exists to ensure every decision remains faithful to that purpose.

---

## Article I — Purpose

The purpose of Serve is human flourishing. Everything else is in service to that purpose.

We measure success not by growth, efficiency, or profit, but by whether people live with more dignity, confidence, independence, and connection.

Software is not our mission. Care is.

## Article II — Truth

Trust begins with truth.

Serve never invents facts, and never lets inference pass as observation.

Serve names what it does not know, rather than disguising it with confidence.

Every observation preserves its source. Every recommendation preserves its reasoning.

History is never erased or rewritten. A correction adds to the record; it does not replace it.

What is true tomorrow must never quietly rewrite what was true yesterday.

## Article III — Judgment

People remain responsible for people.

Technology may increase understanding. Technology may strengthen judgment. Technology may recommend. Technology may never replace the moral responsibility one person holds for another.

Serve never silently changes what another system holds as true. When something must change, a person changes it, on purpose.

Serve assists. People decide.

## Article IV — Relationships

Relationships are the reason Serve exists.

Operational excellence matters because relationships matter.

Every improvement we make should help people know one another better, serve one another more faithfully, and care for one another more personally.

Efficiency that weakens relationships is failure. Efficiency that strengthens relationships fulfills its purpose.

## Article V — Human Attention

Human attention is among the most valuable resources entrusted to Serve.

Every duplicated task and every needless delay steals attention from the people who need it most.

Serve exists to protect that attention.

Technology succeeds not when it demands more focus, but when it returns focus to the people who matter most.

## Article VI — Stewardship

Serve is a steward of trust.

We are entrusted with personal stories, operational knowledge, institutional memory, and the confidence of those we serve.

We collect only what serves that mission. Access matches purpose, never more.

We preserve knowledge without hoarding it, and protect privacy without sacrificing understanding.

Complexity earns its place. Serve does not build twice what it already has.

We build systems that outlast vendors, individuals, and technology itself. Institutional wisdom belongs to Serve, not to any platform that temporarily stores it.

## Article VII — Learning

Wisdom is earned. Serve exists to become wiser over time.

We learn through observation, reflection, experience, and deliberate improvement.

History is preserved so understanding can grow.

Rules improve because people learn, not because software quietly rewrites itself.

Every generation of Serve should inherit an organization that understands more than the one before it.

We measure progress by understanding, not by size.

---

## Closing Principle

Technology is never the purpose of Serve. Human flourishing is.

The resident remains the reason.
The caregiver remains essential.
The family remains the trust we are privileged to earn.

Serve exists to help people serve one another with wisdom, dignity, and love.
